📈 AI Geotechnical Landslide Hazard Index (LHI) Formula

Mathematical formulation combining slope topography, continuous precipitation, and soil shear resistance.

LHI = (0.35 × Slope) + (0.25 × Rainfall) + (0.20 × Soil) + (0.20 × Fault)
• Slope Factor (35%): Derived from ISRO CartoDEM digital elevation model.
• Rainfall Saturation (25%): 72-hour cumulative precipitation from Open-Meteo satellite feeds.
• Soil Shear Resistance (20%): Clay-shale moisture saturation threshold.
• Fault Proximity (20%): Distance to Main Central Thrust (MCT) seismic lineaments.
